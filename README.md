# ✦ DormGlow — Colorful Pinterest-Inspired Dorm & Hostel Glow-Up Studio

> **Pinterest × Dreamy Dorm Decor × Colorful Lifestyle Magazine × Personalized Room Makeover Studio**

DormGlow is a colorful, interactive, lifestyle web application designed to help college and hostel students transform their 10×10 ft. rooms into aesthetic sanctuaries on a student budget.

---

## 🎨 6 Core Features

1. **💸 Visual Room Makeover Budget Studio**
   - Interactive budget slider with live total, spent, and remaining balance calculations.
   - Spending categories (Bedding, Lighting, Wall Decor, Storage & Plants) with checkboxes, price items, and live under/over budget alerts.
   - Quick-start budget challenges: *My Room Under ₹500*, *Dream Dorm Under ₹1,000*, *Cozy Glow-Up Under ₹2,000*, and *Study Desk Refresh*.
   - Save planned makeover directly to your board or send essentials to your shopping list.

2. **✨ Find Your Dorm Aesthetic Quiz**
   - 6 aesthetic visual selection cards:
     - 🕯️ **Clean Girl** (minimalist luxury, crisp linens, warm lamp glow)
     - 🎀 **Coquette** (soft romantic, blush pink, lace ribbons, fairy canopy)
     - 🌿 **Earthy Boho** (terracotta, trailing plants, macramé, warm ochre)
     - 📐 **Minimalist** (functional zen, uncluttered surfaces, neutral tones)
     - ☕ **Dark Academia** (vintage books, espresso tones, antique gold glow)
     - 🎨 **Pinterest Core** (vibrant photo walls, pastel squiggles, playful decor)
   - 5-step visual quiz (aesthetic, color palette swatches, room challenge, budget, transformation zone).
   - Instant personalized results with 4-swatch color palette and matching room inspirations.

3. **📌 Pinterest-Like Save Interactions and Boards**
   - Every inspiration card features an elegant hover/touch **Save** button.
   - Save to existing boards (*My Dream Dorm*, *Under ₹1,000*, *Desk Inspo*) or create new custom boards with 1 click.
   - Visual Boards Drawer displaying collage covers, pin counts, and individual board views.
   - Full backward compatibility with `dg_saved`.

4. **🎨 Interactive Color Studio (Palette Explorer)**
   - 6 curated aesthetic palettes (*Pastel Dreams*, *Earthy & Cozy*, *Pink Paradise*, *Fresh & Sage*, *Warm Neutrals*, *Dark Academia*).
   - Clickable large color swatches with one-click hex copying.
   - **Live Mini-Room SVG Visualizer**: Real-time room mockup that colors walls, bedding, pillows, and lamps according to the active palette.
   - One-click button to filter the gallery for makeovers matching the active palette.

5. **🛏️ My Room Makeover Journey**
   - Upload your current hostel room photo with replace/remove controls and personal notes.
   - Interactive **Before-and-After Draggable Slider** to compare bare rooms against aesthetic glow-ups.
   - Smart Makeover Task Checklist with progress bar and celebration on 100% completion.
   - Transparent, clearly labeled **AI Room Redesign — Coming Soon** preview.

6. **🛍️ Shop the Look / Recreate the Look**
   - Click "Shop the Look" on any inspiration pin to open the detailed recreation modal.
   - Breakdown of essential items with estimated prices in INR.
   - **Affordable Alternatives** for every item (e.g. thrifted dupatta canopy instead of store drape to save money).
   - 1-click addition of items to your Shopping Checklist or Budget Studio.

## 🚀 Running Locally

Serve the application locally with Python:

```bash
python -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000) in your browser.
