Fairy Light Wall Canopy
₹300 2 hours Beginner
Materials Required
LED fairy lights (5m), adhesive command hooks, sheer white fabric or dupatta, small binder clips.
Step-by-Step Instructions
1.Clean wall surface thoroughly with rubbing alcohol and let dry for 5 minutes.
2.Affix two command hooks near the ceiling corners above your bed and wait 1 hour for adhesive bond.
3.Drape sheer fabric across hooks and weave the warm fairy lights through the fabric folds.
⚠️ Safety note: Use low-heat LED fairy lights only. Keep bulbs away from direct contact with paper or heating elements.
